What "AI Readiness" actually means

AI Readiness isn't about whether you use ChatGPT. It's about whether the work you actually do is set up to benefit from AI – so it can save you time, raise the quality of what your practice produces, and give you back the hours that should be going to client conversations and growth.

For that to happen, five things need to be in place:

  • Your data is usable. AI can't help if the information you need lives in someone's head.
  • You know where your time is going. Repetitive tasks you can name are tasks you can delegate.
  • You know your customers. Who they are, what they want, and how to reach them. AI can't help you serve people you don't understand.
  • There's a clear way you do things. AI amplifies what's repeatable. If your methods live in one person's head, there's nothing to amplify yet.
  • You have momentum. You've tried a few things, you're curious, and you want to move. This is the input that makes the rest of the system actually get used.

Where the leverage is

The opportunity areas

Four places where the diagnostic surfaced real leverage, sized to what an advisor practice controls inside a regulated firm. These are observations and directions, not a finished spec – the AI Plan is where they get prioritized, sequenced, and turned into something concrete.

1

Advisor Practice Operating System

Anchor opportunity · Speaks to the magic wand answer

The most direct answer to your magic wand: a documented, AI-assisted operating system for your practice – the SOPs your wand asked for, scoped to what one Edward Jones advisor actually does in a week. Captures the recurring practice routines that Salesforce doesn't structure for you: weekly prospect pipeline review (who's at what stage, what's the next move, when was last contact), monthly COI touch cadence (CPAs, attorneys, estate planners, Chamber connections), quarterly client-review prep workflow drawing on public-information inputs, referral-ask scripts by client segment, annual review documentation. The opportunity is to take the practice-operating layer that lives nowhere today and make it a documented system you can actually run. Lives entirely on your side. Reads from your own Salesforce notes. Never touches account data, holdings, or anything the firm's compliance posture would care about. The exact shape (which routines first, which AI tool, what the rollout looks like) is what the AI Plan would work out.

2

Follow-Up & Referral Engine

Time-leverage opportunity · Closes the headache and the 1/5 referral score

Direct answer to "following up with customers or leads" as the operational headache, and to the 1 out of 5 on referral systems. The opportunity is to compress the follow-up surface and turn the existing book into a referral engine, in the same motion. AI-drafted follow-up cadences for warm prospects who haven't moved (sequenced by stage and time-since-last-touch), existing clients due for review, COIs going dormant, post-meeting thank-yous, post-life-event check-ins. A structured referral-ask system on top: when to ask, what to say, who gets which version of the question, how to track who's referring whom. Inputs are your own Salesforce notes and meeting recaps – nothing leaves your desk that doesn't already live there. The 1/5 referral answer becomes a documented motion, not a hope. The AI Plan would scope which follow-up surfaces matter most to you and how aggressively to compress them.
